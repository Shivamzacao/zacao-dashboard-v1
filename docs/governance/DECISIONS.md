| DEC-018 | Approve the LTV numerator and window for LTV:CAC, and a paid-only CAC scope. LTV is net product revenue earned in a customer's first 90 days, derived from Shopify order history, aggregated as a customer-weighted mean over monthly acquisition cohorts whose 90-day window has fully elapsed; it is withheld below 30 matured customers and warns below 100. Shopify's `amountSpent` field is not the source, because it is lifetime-to-date and cannot express a fixed-age window. CAC is published as `Paid CAC` — paid media spend divided by the first-time customers the ad platforms attribute to it — scoped to paid media only and never presented as a blended company CAC. `conversions` is never the denominator.
