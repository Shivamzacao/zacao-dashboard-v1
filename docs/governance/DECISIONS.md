| DEC-018 | Approve the following formulas and thresholds, unblocking 13 metrics that were `BUSINESS_RULE_REQUIRED`. Actuals are read from Shopify/the manual workbook verbatim; no value is inferred beyond the formula stated. (1) **Revenue vs Plan**: actual = DEC-015 canonical `net_sales`; plan = `Metric_Targets` row matched by `metric_key`, overlapping period, and scope; variance = actual − plan. (2) **Inventory Value**: `SUM(on-hand quantity × total_unit_cost_usd)` per SKU, using the `COGS_By_SKU` row effective for that SKU as of the reporting date; `COGS_By_SKU` is the cost authority, not Shopify. (3) **Sell-Through**: units sold in period ÷ (beginning on-hand + units received in period), per SKU/period. (4) **Runway & Reorder Alert**: daily velocity = trailing-30-day units sold ÷ 30; runway days = available on-hand ÷ daily velocity; alert fires when runway days < average `Production_Orders` lead time + a 14-day safety buffer. (5) **FEFO Visibility**: flags any `Inventory_Lots` row with `status = in_stock` and `best_by_date` within 90 days, ranked soonest-expiring first. (6) **Actual Margin**: (Shopify `net_sales` − `COGS_By_SKU.total_unit_cost_usd` × units sold) ÷ `net_sales`, per period; `COGS_By_SKU` is the cost authority. (7) **Monthly Burn**: `SUM(Finance_Actuals.actual_amount_usd)` for expense categories where `cash_or_accrual = cash`, per month. (8) **Cash Runway**: latest `Cash_Position.cash_balance_usd` ÷ trailing-3-month average monthly burn. (9) **Low Inventory Alert**: fires when available on-hand for a SKU is below its `Metric_Targets` row (`metric_key = inventory.reorder_point`). (10) **Conversion Decline Alert**: fires when the Shopify funnel conversion rate drops more than 20% versus the same-length prior period. (11) **Refund Increase Alert**: fires when returns ÷ gross sales for the period exceeds 2× the trailing-90-day average. (12) **Fulfillment Backlog Alert**: fires when an order remains unfulfilled more than 3 days past its order date. (13) **Realized LTV**: trailing-12-month `SUM(net_sales)` per customer ÷ distinct customer count, on a revenue basis (no cost allocation). Explicitly deferred, not covered by this decision: Business Health Score, Executive Recommendations, CAC/ROAS/LTV:CAC (need a Klaviyo attribution-window decision beyond this scope), and the Fairafric Rebate (no formula basis exists yet — needs the actual rebate agreement terms). | Approved by ZACAO 2026-08-10 | Business-rule activation review |
